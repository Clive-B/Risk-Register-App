NCA GRC System User Manual

Secure, role-aware guidance for identity, onboarding, risk governance, approvals, treatment, compliance and assistant use.

Current reference: After sign-in, open System Manual from the navigation menu. This attached PDF is the onboarding edition; the in-system manual reflects later releases.

1. Purpose and authority

NCA GRC records division risks, assessments, treatments, approvals, compliance evidence and executive reporting. It preserves the source of material figures and the officer responsible for each action. System and assistant outputs support work; authorised NCA officers retain decision authority.

2. Accounts and personnel

Each governed role has a permanent organizational role account, separate from the employee currently assigned to it. From User Management → Map NCA Accounts, an Administrator records the holder's official NCA email, effective date and reason.

Replacement

  • The former holder becomes inactive and their sessions are revoked.
  • Historical actions remain attributed to the former holder.
  • Unfinished assigned actions move to the new holder.
  • The new holder receives secure onboarding and must change the temporary password.

Administrator delegation

Use Create Administrator only after confirming the person's identity, official NCA email and authority-wide business need. The new administrator follows the same first-login password process.

Clear Holder

Use Clear Holder when a role account must become unassigned. It removes active access and revokes sessions without deleting the permanent role account or rewriting historic attribution.

3. Onboarding and passwords

The onboarding email includes the login link, a temporary password, this PDF and a link to the current online manual. The user must change the temporary password immediately after first sign-in.

The change screen displays the active server policy. The standard production policy requires at least 12 characters, including uppercase and lowercase letters, a number and a special character. Each password field has a user-controlled Show option. Reveal a password only when the screen cannot be observed.

Grouped onboarding

Administrators may defer individual messages while filling several role accounts, review the completed holders, then select Send Group Onboarding. Each person receives a separate message and temporary password.

4. Email and Communication Centre

System email is sent through the configured Risk Management System Gmail service account. Its app password is encrypted in system settings and is intentionally not displayed after saving.

Use Communication Centre to review queued, sent and failed messages and retry a failure after correcting its cause. "Queued" means the application accepted the message; it is not proof of mailbox arrival. Onboarding and password messages are restricted to official @nca.org.gh recipients.

5. Risk intake and assessment

A Risk Focal Person retains direct access to Risk Register and Work Plan upload. An Administrator can also stage approved workbooks supplied by several divisions. For Administrator-assisted upload, workbook likelihood, impact and score are shown as Focal · Pending Confirmation, never as System figures.

The assigned Focal Person reviews or edits every row and submits the complete division register once to the Risk Champion. Champion and Chairman decisions remain separate, attributable governance stages.

5A. Multiple risk entry

Risk Focal Persons and Risk Champions can complete one manual risk, select Add Risk to Batch, and continue entering additional risks. Review the queue and use Submit Batch to send all queued records. Every risk is validated separately and keeps its own identifier, attachments, workflow and audit history.

5B. Treatment oversight

Risk Champions and the Risk Committee Chairman can filter overdue treatment plans and order treatments by due date, progress or recorded completion time. Completion evidence remains required, and the recorded completion timestamp supports chronological oversight without changing approval authority.

5C. Chairman quarterly dashboard

On Board Dashboard, choose a reporting year and Quarter 1-4 or the full year. The dashboard recalculates governed figures for the selected period and groups risks by approved category, including High, Medium, Low and closed totals. Use Print / Save Dashboard to produce a quarterly copy.

6. Assistant

The assistant can explain workflows, summarise authorised work and identify supporting records. It cannot approve, submit, assign, delete or otherwise change official records. Recommendations are advisory and must be checked against source records by an authorised officer.

General and System Help exclude private work context by default. Personalised modes require the user to select authorised work context, while server-side identity and scope controls continue to apply. Connected AI and speech processing remain subject to NCA approval for purpose, region, retention, security and cross-border handling.

7. Key pages by role

  • System Administrator: User Management, Create Administrator, Account Mapping, grouped onboarding, Communication Centre, Division Risk Uploads, settings and audit logs.
  • Risk Focal Person: direct workbook upload, Administrator-Staged Risks, multiple manual risk entry, work plans, assigned approvals and treatments.
  • Risk Champion: division approvals, multiple manual risk entry, assessments, treatment oversight and reports.
  • Risk Committee Chairman: quarterly and category-grouped Board Dashboard, enterprise analytics, high-risk reviews and final governed decisions.

8. Secure operating guidance

  • Use only official @nca.org.gh employee addresses.
  • Use least privilege and grant administrator access only for a confirmed need.
  • Review onboarding delivery status; regenerating a temporary password revokes existing sessions.
  • Record reasons for adjustments and personnel replacement.
  • Verify supporting evidence before approval or completion.
  • Preserve the audit trail and never rename one person's record to represent another.

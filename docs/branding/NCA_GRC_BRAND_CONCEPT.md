# NCA GRC Brand Concept 01 — Governed Convergence

Status: Product-owner review

Formal product name: **National Communications Authority Governance, Risk and Compliance Platform**  
Product name: **NCA GRC**

## Brand architecture

NCA GRC uses an endorsed institutional lockup. The official NCA emblem remains unchanged and appears beside, not inside, the NCA GRC product mark. The product mark does not represent a new statutory authority or replace the official emblem.

## Symbol meaning

- Shield: governance, institutional protection and assurance.
- Three converging paths: Governance, Risk and Compliance as distinct but connected disciplines.
- Three source nodes: attributable inputs and preserved provenance.
- Central approval node and check: accountable human decision-making.
- Forward shield form: continuous monitoring and institutional improvement.

## Approved concept colours

- NCA Navy: `#003B6F`
- Governance Blue: `#0871B6`
- Ghana Gold accent: `#FFC72C`
- Assurance Green: `#21A366`
- White: `#FFFFFF`
- Supporting slate: `#587083`

Red is reserved for risk warnings and is not a dominant brand colour.

## Integrity rules

- Do not alter, redraw, recolour, crop or merge the official NCA emblem into the product symbol.
- Do not use the product mark alone where formal institutional attribution is required.
- Do not add unapproved cultural, governmental or ceremonial symbols.
- Do not stretch, rotate, outline or apply decorative effects to the mark.
- Preserve adequate clear space and contrast at every size.

## Proposed variants

1. Primary institutional lockup for PRDs, reports and sign-in.
2. Compact NCA GRC lockup for dashboards and navigation.
3. Standalone product mark for favicon and application icon.
4. Monochrome mark for formal and grayscale output.

No live application branding changes should be made until product-owner approval is recorded.

How this page works

Select the template that matches your register. The appropriate rule-driven correction engine will preserve the original values, explain every proposed correction and require your confirmation before any risk is admitted.

  1. Download and complete one of the two governed templates.
  2. Upload it under the matching template card.
  3. Review and edit the proposed corrections, then confirm each risk.
  4. Admit the corrected risks. They will appear on the Consolidated Risk Register for all subsequent editing and review.
